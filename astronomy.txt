# Introduction to Astronomy

Astronomy is the scientific study of celestial objects, space, and the universe. It helps us understand planets, stars, galaxies, black holes, and other objects found beyond Earth.

## 1. The Solar System

The Solar System consists of the Sun and all the objects that orbit around it. There are eight planets in our Solar System: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.

The Sun is a star located at the center of the Solar System. It provides heat and light that support life on Earth.

## 2. Stars

Stars are massive balls of hot gases, primarily hydrogen and helium. They produce energy through nuclear fusion, a process in which hydrogen atoms combine to form helium.

The Sun is the closest star to Earth. Other stars appear small because they are extremely far away from our planet.

## 3. Galaxies

A galaxy is a huge collection of stars, gas, dust, and dark matter held together by gravity.

The Milky Way is the galaxy that contains our Solar System. It is a spiral galaxy containing billions of stars.

## 4. Black Holes

A black hole is a region in space where gravity is extremely strong. Nothing, not even light, can escape once it crosses the event horizon.

Black holes can form when massive stars collapse under their own gravity.

## 5. The Moon

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It revolves around Earth and takes approximately 27.3 days to complete one orbit relative to distant stars.

The Moon does not produce its own light. It reflects sunlight, which makes it visible from Earth.

## 6. Space Exploration

Space exploration involves studying outer space using satellites, telescopes, spacecraft, and robotic missions.

NASA, ISRO, and ESA are some well-known space agencies. India's Chandrayaan missions have contributed significantly to lunar exploration.

## Conclusion

Astronomy helps scientists understand the origin, structure, and evolution of the universe. Modern telescopes and space missions continue to discover new information about distant planets, stars, and galaxies.
